ACCESSING OR CORRECTING PERSONAL DATA

You may access or correct your name, birthday, phone numbers, shipping and billing addresses or other Personal Data by logging in to your user account on our Platform.

For Personal Data that cannot be corrected by you by logging in to your user account on our Platform, you may request to access and correct such Personal Data by submitting a written request to us by emailing our Data Protection Officer at DPO@YSG.ASIA. We may need to request additional information from you to confirm your identity before providing the access or making the correction.

We will do our best to respond to Personal Data access and correction requests within twenty-one (21) business days from the receipt date of such requests. Where we are unable to meet this timeline, we will provide you with the next earliest time and date which we can provide the information or make the correction.

We reserve the right to charge a reasonable administrative fee for retrieving your Personal Data records and/or handling and processing your Personal Data access request. If so, we will inform you of the estimated administrative fee. We will only process your request when you agree to pay the fee and we are not under any obligation to accede to your request if otherwise. 

We will take steps to send your corrected Personal Data to every other person or entity to which such Personal Data was disclosed by YSG within the year after the date of the correction, unless such person or entity does not need the corrected Personal Data for any legal or business purpose.
