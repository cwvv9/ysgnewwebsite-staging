The Technology Behind Our Services

Powering a truly omnichannel ecosystem with technology that seamlessly connects marketplaces, stores, warehouses, and logistics — unifying operations, inventory, and fulfilment across every channel for a seamless customer experience.

Online Marketplaces
Provides an integrated marketplace interface covering Product Listing & Catalogue, Seller Centre / Stock Level Display, Order Placement, Campaign / Flash Sale Configuration, FBS / FBL Fulfilment, and AWB / Shipping Label Generation. It enables products to be published, stock levels to be displayed, customer orders to be captured, promotions to be configured, and fulfilment documentation to be generated across connected marketplaces.
OMS (Order Management System)
Acts as the central order orchestration layer, covering Order Receiving, Safety Stock Configuration, Order Status Updates, SoftKitting, Order Processing & Routing, Campaign Mode, SKU / Product Mapping, Channel Stock Allocations, GWP Settings & Rules, Inventory Sync, and Returns & Cancellation Handling. It coordinates incoming orders, applies business rules, allocates inventory, manages product and campaign requirements, synchronises stock and order statuses, and controls returns and cancellations across sales channels.
WMS (Warehouse Management System)
Manages end-to-end warehouse operations through Inbound / GRN, ASN Generation & Putaway, Inventory Management, Pick & Pack, Batch / Wave / AGG Picking, Kitting & Bundling, RF Scanning, DO Creation & Commitment, Stock Sync to OMS, and Outbound / Dispatch. It controls the movement of goods from receiving and storage through picking, packing, inventory control, order preparation, and final dispatch.
TMS (Transportation Management System)
Manages end-to-end transportation operations through Order Consolidation, Route Planning & Optimization, Carrier Selection & Allocation, Freight Booking, Shipment Tracking, Proof of Delivery, and Freight Cost Management. It controls the movement of goods from origin to destination through planning, dispatch, in-transit visibility, delivery confirmation, and cost settlement.
API
Client Warehouse
The client's own warehouse operation, integrated with the Client POS, manages locally fulfilled stock outside YSG's WMS while keeping inventory levels and stock movements synchronised.
Client POS (Point-of-Sale) System
The client's Point-of-Sale (POS) system, integrated via the Y3 API, exchanges data with the OMS to keep orders, fulfilment statuses, store transactions, and stock movements synchronised across channels.
Client ERP
The client's Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system, such as SAP or Oracle, receives order, transaction, and inventory data from the Client POS for financial recording, inventory valuation, and reporting, keeping core business operations aligned with actual sales activity.
