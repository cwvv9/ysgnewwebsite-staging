Warehouse &
Fulfilment

YCH's B2C Warehouse Fulfilment solution provides end-to-end inventory management, order processing, packing, shipping, and returns services, enabling brands to deliver fast, accurate, and seamless customer experiences across every sales channel.

Fusionaris

Infinite Possibilities

As the centrepiece of Supply Chain City, Fusionaris® is a world-first, patented innovation that integrates a 5-storey ramp-up warehouse and a 50-metre-high automated storage and retrieval system (ASRS).

By merging manual handling with high-speed robotics, it maximises land-use density and eliminates operational bottlenecks. Fusionaris® offers a scalable, future-proof platform that enables infinite possibilities for businesses to innovate and grow within the global supply chain.

Warehouse Management System (WMS)

Once orders are released from the OMS, our advanced Warehouse Management System orchestrates every warehouse operation with precision and accuracy.

Goods receiving and put-away
Inventory storage and location management
FIFO/FEFO inventory control
Wave, batch, or single-order picking
Packing and quality inspection
Carrier integration and shipment confirmation
Returns processing and inventory reconciliation

Barcode-driven workflows and real-time inventory tracking ensure high order accuracy while maximising warehouse productivity.
