Warehouse &
Fulfilment

YCH's B2C Warehouse Fulfilment solution provides end-to-end inventory management, order processing, packing, shipping, and return services, enabling brands to deliver fast, accurate, and seamless customer experiences across every sales channel.

Warehouse Management
System (WMS)

Once orders are released from the OMS, our advanced Warehouse Management System orchestrates every warehouse operation with precision and accuracy.

Goods receiving and put-away
FIFO/FEFO inventory control
Wave, batch, or single-order picking
Picking and packing inspection
Shipping label generation
Carrier integration and shipment confirmation
Returns processing and inventory reconciliation

Barcode-driven workflows and real-time inventory tracking ensure high order accuracy while maximizing warehouse productivity.

VIETNAM SuperPort

With an investment of over US$270 million, Vietnam SuperPort™ is a transformative logistics infrastructure project—the largest of its kind led by a Singaporean company in Vietnam currently underway.


It aims to enhance Vietnam's integration into global supply chains, reduce logistics costs, and strengthen the country's competitive edge in international trade.

Joint Venture Between YCH Group
(Singapore) and T&T Group (Vietnam)

YCH Group is Singapore's largest homegrown logistics supply chain solutions company.

T&T Group is among the top 10 largest private multi-sector economic groups in Vietnam.

The project connects major logistics routes and strengthens regional distribution capabilities.

About Vietnam SuperPort™

  • 68 ha multimodal logistics hub in Phu Tho province
  • Designed cargo capacity of up to 1M TEUs
  • Highly aligned with ASEAN Smart Logistics Network (ASLN)
